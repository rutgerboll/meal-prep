preserved to keep task provenance, results, and design reasoning available.

current app instruction.

- `CONTRACT.md`, `README.md`, `KICKOFF.md`, and `PRINCIPLES.md`: old program rules,
  prompts, and scaffold.

- `knowledge/loops.md`: proposed management loops, never adopted as a
  product requirement.
- `tasks/T0.4-original.md`: original smoke-suite brief, including the retired
  orchestration setup. The current brief keeps the useful test goal.

Current app work starts at `../../README.md` and `../../TASKS.md`. The actual
task briefs, findings, app code, design system, and meal library remain in their
working locations.
