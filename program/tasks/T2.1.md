> Read README.md and CONTRACT.md first. Sharpest rules here: §5 — you may not generalize

## Why this task exists

ROADMAP #7. The two halves of the app disagree about how long a plan can be: the **grocery
engine already handles 3–4 week plans** — it splits fresh produce per the week it's used so
nothing wilts, and generalizes past two — while the **in-app suggester and manual builder
only make 2-week plans**. Longer plans exist today only if Claude generates them and they
sync in, which means the in-app path silently caps a capability the rest of the app has.

## Objective

Let the in-app "Suggest a plan" and the manual builder produce plans of 3 and 4 weeks that
hold up to the same standard as the 2-week plan: cuisine spread maintained, no two
same-cuisine nights adjacent, liked recipes favored, base capacity respected, and the
resulting grocery list splitting correctly per week.

## In scope

- `app/js/plan-builder.js` and `app/js/screen-plan.js` — the suggester's balancing and the
  builder's UI for a variable number of weeks.
- The week-2-from-the-freezer mirroring rule: decide and document what it *means* at 3–4
  weeks. Mirroring week 1 three times is not obviously right; neither is cooking fresh
  every week. This is the real design question in the task, not the loop bound.
- Base capacity across a longer horizon (a base's portions are bought once; a 4-week plan
  may need more bases, not more finishes off the same base).
- Confirming the grocery split actually holds at 3 and 4 weeks rather than trusting
  ROADMAP's claim that it generalizes.

## Out of scope

- The Claude-side `meal-planner` skill. It can already make long plans; this is the in-app path.
- Live two-way sync (ROADMAP #8) — explicitly deferred, and nothing here should assume it.
- Redesigning the Plan screen. Borderless hairline rows under `.ssec` overlines is settled
  (`DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`); extend the pattern, don't revisit it.

## Starting points

## Known traps for THIS task

- **`sw.js` CACHE bump.** Every file you'll touch is precached, and `scripts/pre-commit`
  only guards `index.html` — a JS-only change commits clean and ships stale (GOTCHAS).
- Portion-aware availability caps a recipe by how many times it's planned. Over 4 weeks the
  interaction between capacity, mirroring, and "used up" is where this will actually break.
- Existing installs carry plans in IndexedDB. A longer plan must not corrupt or drop a
  2-week plan already stored — test against a populated DB, not a fresh one (CONTRACT §5).
- `build_seed.py` needs `MP_BASE_DIR` from a worktree (GOTCHAS).
- The grocery split's "Buy for week 2 (around …)" labelling has to stay legible at 4 weeks.

## Deliverable

`findings/T2.1.md` per CONTRACT §6, plus the shipped change with a bumped `sw.js` CACHE,
plus the mirroring decision written down (`knowledge/` or `ROADMAP.md`) so the next session
doesn't relitigate it.

## Done when

A 3-week and a 4-week plan both build via the suggester **and** the manual builder, each
produces a correctly split grocery list, a pre-existing 2-week plan survives the upgrade
untouched, the console is clean on the Plan and List screens, and `sw.js` is bumped.
